Privacy Policy

This policy explains how NullPointer Exception Handler (the â€œBotâ€), operated for the Hack Theory Discord server, processes information when members participate in quizzes or use its commands.

Short version: the Bot does not read ordinary messages or private DMs. Quiz buttons, tutor prompts, and security reports are processed only when a member deliberately uses those features.

Information the Bot processes

  • Discord user ID, current username, and current server display name.
  • Quiz ID, selected answer, whether the answer was correct, and answer timestamp.
  • Aggregate statistics such as total answers, correct answers, current streak, and best streak.
  • XP totals and the count and label of any random, non-cash quiz bonus.
  • Text deliberately submitted to /ask or /fact-check, which is sent to Cloudflare Workers AI to generate a response. Prompts are rate-limited; the Bot does not intentionally retain their text after the response.
  • Reports deliberately submitted with /report-security, including reporter ID, reported-member ID, report text, timestamp, and review status.
  • The configured Discord server and channel IDs needed to deliver quizzes and staff logs.
  • Minimal operational errors and request metadata processed by the hosting providers for security and reliability.

Information the Bot does not request

The Bot does not request or intentionally collect ordinary message content, direct messages, email addresses, linked Discord connections, presence, friend relationships, voice or video activity, contacts, passwords, payment information, or precise location. All privileged Gateway intents are disabled.

How information is used

Information is used to score quizzes and rewards, show statistics, provide requested tutor responses, deliver educational and CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities alerts, privately queue suspicious-contact reports for human staff review, diagnose failures, and protect the service from abuse.

Storage and retention

Quiz-level answer records and closed quiz snapshots are retained for up to 30 days by default. Security reports are retained for up to 90 days for staff review and abuse prevention. Aggregate leaderboard statistics are retained until member deletion, decommissioning, or administrative deletion. Temporary deletion confirmations expire after approximately ten minutes.

Deletion and member choices

Use /delete-my-data in Hack Theory to review a confirmation and permanently delete your retained quiz answers and leaderboard profile. You can also ask Hack Theory staff for help if the command is unavailable. Deletion does not remove Discord's own message records or third-party infrastructure logs controlled by Discord, Cloudflare, or GitHub.

Service providers

Discord delivers application interactions and messages. Cloudflare hosts the handler, state coordinator, and opt-in AI tutor. GitHub Pages hosts this site, and CISA's official GitHub mirror supplies the public Known Exploited Vulnerabilities feed. These providers may process network and account metadata under their own terms.

Sharing

Correct-participant results and deliberately submitted security reports are shared only in the configured staff-log channel. Public leaderboards can show display names and quiz statistics. A tutor response is public only when the member explicitly selects that option. Information is not sold.

Security

The Bot verifies Discord's Ed25519 request signatures, rejects stale or invalid interaction requests, stores the bot token only as an encrypted hosting secret, requests least-privilege Discord permissions, and avoids privileged Gateway access. No system is perfectly secure, but the service is designed to minimize collected data and limit access.

Children

The Bot is not directed to children below the minimum age required to use Discord in their country. If information is believed to have been collected from an ineligible user, contact Hack Theory staff so it can be removed.

Changes

This policy may be updated when functionality, providers, or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top will be revised, and material changes may be announced in Hack Theory.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Hack Theory server staff through Discord. Do not send passwords, bot tokens, or other secrets.
